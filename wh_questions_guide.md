
# USOS: SABER DE?

## WH- Questions (Palabras interrogativas)

| Tipo de información                | WH- Word | Traducción   | Pronunciación |
|-----------------------------------|----------|--------------|----------------|
| Personas                          | WHO      | ¿Quién?      | *ju*           |
| Información general               | WHAT     | ¿Qué?        | *guat*         |
| Tiempo (fechas, momentos, etc.)   | WHEN     | ¿Cuándo?     | *wen*          |
| Lugares                           | WHERE    | ¿Dónde?      | *wer*          |
| Causas / motivos                  | WHY      | ¿Por qué?    | *wai*          |
| Elección entre opciones           | WHICH    | ¿Cuál?       | *wich*         |
| Posesión                          | WHOSE    | ¿De quién?   | *jus*          |
| Proceso / manera / forma         | HOW      | ¿Cómo?       | *jau*          |

---

## Ejemplos con **WHOSE**

- **Whose phone is ringing?**  
- **Whose books are these on the table?**  
- **Whose book is this on the table?**  
- **Whose does that coin belong to?** *(Presente)*

---

## Ejemplos con **HOW**

- **How does she do her homework?**  
  (¿Cómo hace ella la tarea?)  
  → **Presente**: *do* (plurales), *does* (singulares)  
- **How did she do her homework?**  
  (¿Cómo hizo ella la tarea?) → **Pasado**: *did*

---

## Verbo ESTAR (to be)

| Tiempo   | Singular             | Plural              |
|----------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Pasado   | **was** (I, she, he, it) | **were** (you, we, they) |

---

## Ejemplos con **WHY**

- **Why do you break the glass?**  
  (¿Por qué rompes el vaso?) → Presente  
- **Why did you break the glass?**  
  (¿Por qué rompiste el vaso?) → Pasado  
- **Why did you cheat me?**  
  (¿Por qué me engañaste?)  
- **Why do you cheat me?**  
  (¿Por qué me engañas?)

---

## Ejemplos con **WHICH / WHAT**

- **Which is your favourite food?**  
  Pizza, hot dogs, hamburguesas, sushi  
- **What technology do you like?**  
  (¿Qué tecnología te gusta?)  
- **Which technology do you like?**  
  (¿Cuál tecnología te gusta? → Con opciones)

---

## Ejemplos con **WHO**

- **Who is your favourite artist?**  
  → *My favourite artist is Videoclub*  
- **Who is Cristian's favourite artist?**  
  → *Cristian's favourite artist is Videoclub*  
- **Who are Cristian's favourite artists?**  
  → *Cristian's favourite artists are Ricardo Arjona and Kina*  

---

## Verbos y Pronombres

| Pronombre       | Verbo To Be | Significado       |
|------------------|---------------|------------------|
| I, He, She, It   | am, is        | soy, es          |
| We, You, They    | are           | somos, son       |

---

## Ejemplos con **WHEN**

- **When is the exam?**  
- **When's the exam?**  
- **When is Xavier's birthday?**  
  → *My birthday is in April / ON April 15th*  
- **When was the exam?**

---

## Ejemplos con **WHERE**

- **Where do you live?**  
  (¿Dónde vives?)  
- **Where does she live?**  
  (¿Dónde vive ella?)  
- **Where did she live?**  
  (¿Dónde vivía ella?)

---

## Ejemplos con **WHY (Presente / Pasado / Progresivo)**

- **Why did Julieta play with my feelings?**  
  → *Julieta played with my feelings*  
- **Why does Julieta play with my feelings?**  
  → *Julieta plays with my feelings*  
- **Why is Julieta playing with my feelings?**  
  → *Julieta is playing with my feelings*

---

## Ejemplos con **WHICH**

- **Which is your favourite programming language?**  
  → *My favourite programming language is JavaScript*  
- **Which programming language do you prefer / like / enjoy?**

---

## Ejemplos con **WHOSE (posesión)**

- **Whose is that red car?**  
  → *That red car belongs to Xavier*

---

## Ejemplos con **HOW**

- **How are you?**  
  → *I'm happy, I'm depressed, I'm angry, I'm hungry*  
- **How is she doing the work?** → (Progresivo)  
- **How does she do the work?** → (Hábito)  

---

## Ejemplos con **WHAT**

- **What are you doing now?**  
  → *I'm studying English*  
- **What do you do in your free time?**  
  → *I study English*  
- **What did you do in your free time during vacations?**  
  → *I watched series and played games*
